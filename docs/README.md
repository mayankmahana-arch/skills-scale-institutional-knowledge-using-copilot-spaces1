# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder contains the shared processes, roles, and practices used by OctoAcme to plan, deliver, release, and continuously improve cross-functional work.

## Project management process at a glance

OctoAcme follows an iterative lifecycle:

1. **Initiation** — Validate the business need, define a measurable goal and success criteria, identify stakeholders, outline milestones, risks, and resources, and make the decision to proceed to planning.
2. **Planning** — Turn the approved initiative into a prioritized, estimated backlog with acceptance criteria. Define the Definition of Done, release plan, milestones, dependencies, risks, and QA approach.
3. **Execution and tracking** — Deliver small increments through the project board and pull request workflow. Use standups, delivery syncs, demos, CI checks, testing, dashboards, and escalation paths to maintain progress and quality.
4. **Risk and communication management** — Maintain a risk register, monitor dependencies, share regular status updates, and escalate blockers through the team, Project Manager, Product Lead, and sponsor as appropriate.
5. **Release and deployment** — Confirm acceptance criteria, CI and security checks, release notes, smoke tests, and rollback plans before deploying. Verify the deployment afterward and communicate the release to stakeholders and support.
6. **Closeout and continuous improvement** — Hold a retrospective after each sprint, release, milestone, or incident. Capture learnings, assign action items, measure their impact, and feed improvements back into the backlog and process documentation.

The overview and lifecycle documents explain how the pieces fit together. The initiation and planning guides establish the work, execution and risk guides support delivery, the release guide governs deployment, and the retrospective guide closes the loop. The roles and personas document clarifies responsibilities across the team.

## How to use these documents

Start with the overview to understand the operating model. During initiation and planning, use the corresponding checklists and templates to establish a ready-to-deliver project. During execution, keep the project board, risk register, status updates, and quality checks current. Before release, complete the deployment checklist and rollback planning. After a sprint, release, milestone, or incident, record the retrospective outcomes and track the resulting action items. Keep the relevant project artifacts in the repository as the single source of truth.
