# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Product Owner / Business Sponsor

### Role Summary
Product Owners or Business Sponsors define the value and strategic intent of the work. They align delivery decisions with business goals, customer needs, and stakeholder expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Prioritize initiatives and features based on business value and customer impact
- Clarify scope, success metrics, and trade-offs for delivery teams
- Validate that outcomes meet organizational goals and user needs
- Sponsor decisions that affect timelines, budgets, and strategic alignment
- Work with Project Managers and Product Managers to resolve escalations and competing priorities

### Goals
- Ensure the team is delivering the highest-value work
- Keep the project aligned with business priorities and stakeholder expectations
- Improve clarity on ownership, outcomes, and trade-offs

### Typical Communication
- Weekly business updates and roadmap reviews
- Decision briefs, prioritization conversations, and stakeholder check-ins
- Approval of scope and milestone readiness

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Product Managers to shape demand and prioritize the backlog
- Collaborates with Project Managers on milestones, dependency management, and stakeholder communication
- Provides context and decisions to Developers and cross-functional teams when trade-offs are required

---

## Delivery Manager / Team Lead

### Role Summary
Delivery Managers or Team Leads coordinate execution across the team and keep work flowing from planning through completion. They support delivery health, team alignment, and dependency management.

### Responsibilities
- Sequence work, manage priorities, and monitor delivery progress
- Remove blockers and coordinate dependencies across teams or stakeholders
- Facilitate planning, standups, and cross-functional coordination
- Support team health, capacity planning, and workload balancing
- Partner with Project Managers and functional leads to maintain forecast accuracy

### Goals
- Improve delivery predictability and throughput
- Keep teams focused on the right priorities at the right time
- Reduce friction caused by blockers, hand-offs, or unclear ownership

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and weekly delivery reviews
- Dependency tracking and sprint or milestone updates
- Cross-team coordination discussions and escalation notes

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to translate backlog items into execution plans and daily focus
- Coordinates with Project Managers on milestones, risks, and communication cadence
- Aligns with Product Owners and Product Managers on priority shifts and trade-offs

---

## Risk and Dependency Manager

### Role Summary
Risk and Dependency Managers identify, assess, and escalate operational and delivery risks. They help ensure issues are visible early and addressed before they derail project outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Track known risks, assumptions, issues, and dependencies across the project
- Assess impact, likelihood, and mitigation strategies for critical blockers
- Escalate unresolved risks to the correct decision-makers or stakeholders
- Maintain a shared risk register and dependency view for the project team
- Work with Project Managers and Delivery Leads to reduce delivery uncertainty

### Goals
- Improve visibility into project risks and dependencies
- Enable proactive mitigation before issues become blockers
- Support better decision-making across the project lifecycle

### Typical Communication
- Risk review meetings and issue tracking updates
- Dependency and escalation reports for leadership or stakeholders
- Recommendations for mitigation and contingency planning

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Supports Project Managers by strengthening risk reporting and mitigation planning
- Collaborates with Developers and Team Leads to understand technical and execution risks
- Provides Product and Business stakeholders with clear information about trade-offs and potential impacts

---

## Change and Communication Lead

### Role Summary
Change and Communication Leads ensure that project stakeholders receive clear, timely, and consistent information. They support adoption, readiness, and alignment to reduce confusion during transitions.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain communication plans for stakeholders, teams, and leadership
- Coordinate updates on timelines, milestones, decisions, and dependencies
- Support change readiness by identifying impacts, training needs, and adoption concerns
- Help translate technical or project status into clear language for non-technical audiences
- Partner with Project Managers to ensure messaging is consistent and aligned

### Goals
- Improve stakeholder confidence and engagement
- Reduce confusion, resistance, or misalignment during change initiatives
- Ensure communication supports successful adoption and delivery outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings, executive updates, and project newsletters
- Change readiness planning and communication calendars
- Status updates tailored to different audiences and concerns

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to maintain consistent messaging and communication cadence
- Collaborates with Product Owners and leadership to share business context and ensure expectations are clear
- Coordinates with Developers and Delivery Leads to communicate progress, impacts, and release readiness

---

## Quality and Governance Partner

### Role Summary
Quality and Governance Partners review project health, process compliance, and quality expectations. They help ensure work is consistent with standards, controls, and success criteria.

### Responsibilities
- Review project delivery against defined quality, governance, and process standards
- Validate that work meets acceptance criteria, compliance expectations, and operating principles
- Support decision checkpoints and governance reviews across the project lifecycle
- Identify gaps in process or quality practices and recommend improvements
- Partner with Project Managers and functional leads to maintain accountability and traceability

### Goals
- Improve confidence in project quality and governance outcomes
- Reduce rework caused by unclear standards or weak process adherence
- Maintain consistent control over risk, quality, and decision-making

### Typical Communication
- Governance review discussions and quality checkpoint meetings
- Recommendations, audit notes, and escalation summaries
- Standards and policy guidance for project teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to reinforce quality standards and validation expectations
- Collaborates with Project Managers to monitor compliance and decision quality
- Supports Product Owners and stakeholders by clarifying whether proposed outcomes meet agreed standards and priorities

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These roles are intended to complement the existing project management personas by clarifying accountability, cross-functional collaboration, and escalation paths.
